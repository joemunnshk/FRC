# Collector Crypt brand — extracted reference

Sources: "Collector Crypt Brand Kit #1" (22-page deck, pulled from
~/Downloads) and computed styles from collectorcrypt.com. Colours below were
sampled pixel-by-pixel from the deck, not eyeballed.

## Typography (stated in the deck)

| Role | Face | The deck's words |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / display | **Perfectly Nineties** | "a modern serif built for retro souls… the tight, editorial feel of '90s typography" |
| Body | **Inter** | "the workhorse behind every word" |
| Accent | **Supply Mono** | "used exclusively in capitals… labels, tags, and supporting text" |

Perfectly Nineties and PP Supply Mono are Pangram Pangram commercial fonts and
need a webfont licence. Free stand-ins, both already loaded on
collectorcrypt.com itself: **Instrument Serif** for the display face,
**Space Mono** for the accent mono. Inter is free on Google Fonts.

## Colour

Page black `#101010` (sampled from the deck background).
Surfaces from the live site: `#1B1B1D` panel, `#161618` raised, `#0E0E0F` page.
Lines `rgba(117,117,117,0.5)` and `#44444B`.
Text white, secondary `#9F9FA8`.
Live-site accents: mint `#2EF098` (prices), orange `#FF9D1C`.
Mono label amber `#F7B70D` — sampled from the deck's own gradient.

### The signature gradient

Sampled across the deck's diagonal band, blue → red:

`#0D57FB` `#0B82F9` `#49BBF4` `#F3F5E8` `#FDF5D6` `#F3C309` `#F7B70D` `#EC7808` `#D4090B`

It appears as a diagonal band, as vertical stripes behind the wordmark, and as
the monogram's outline fill. Works on black and on white — the deck shows both.

## Shape and layout

Radii in use, by frequency: 4px (dominant), 2px, 12px, 8px, 16px, 999px pills.
The marketplace grid separates tiles with 1px hairlines over a lighter backdrop,
so the gallery reads as a ruled table rather than floating cards.
Tile order: image, then uppercase mono meta line, then a bold Inter title, then
a mono price in mint.
Posters carry a three-column mono footer rule:
`COLLECTOR CRYPT 2026 · THE NEW WAY OF TRADING · ALL RIGHTS RESERVED`.
Everything prints over a fine paper grain.

## Voice

"Collector Crypt is a true collector which love for the game started in the 90s.
Its voice bridges retro tech enthusiasts and modern collectors. The tone brings
nostalgic yet forward-thinking, with the mix of inovation and passion for
collecting." (deck, verbatim including its typos)

Headline examples: "Catch them all", "The art of collecting",
"The vintage trading experience".
